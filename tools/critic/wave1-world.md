## Ranked issues

1. **Lake shores are vertical walls; the Vierwaldstättersee is a trench** — evidence: `lake-overview-seelisberg.png`, `flyover-streaming.png`; P1 shore step 15 m outside the polygons mean 80–93 m (max 338 m), P6 70.7 m step in 10 m. Fix: after step 3 in `buildHeightGrid` (`heightmodel.ts:211`) add a shore pass: for every cell with `d = polygonSdf(x,z,lake.poly) ∈ (0, D]` (D ≈ 600 m for the Vierwaldstättersee basins, 300 m for the small lakes) set `h = lerp(lake.levelGameH + valleyProfile(d, 0, SHORE_PROFILE), h, smoothstep(0, D, d))` with `SHORE_PROFILE = { shape:'wideU', halfWidth: 25, influence: D, riseRate: 120 }` — i.e. treat the lake polygon as a corridor whose floor is the water level. This also fixes the Ägerisee slab (P1: terrain 26 m *below* the lake outside its polygon) and the Lauerzersee/Sarnersee. Keep the real Axen cliff by giving the `urnersee` east edge a steeper profile (`riseRate` 250) — but still continuous, never a step. Add a full-res test: max |Δh| per 10 m across any shore ≤ 6 m.
2. **Roads/rivers are stamped as discs around sparse spline samples; passes are 85° walls and `road` is 0.0 % of the map** — evidence: P3, P5, P7 (Morgarten pillar `65→102→64`), census. Fix in `heightmodel.ts:158-209`: replace the per-sample disc loops with distance-to-polyline (`@core/math` already exports `polylineDistance`/`segmentDistance`): for each corridor, rasterise a bounding band of width `influence` around every *segment*, compute `d` to the segment and the arc-length-interpolated floor height, keep nearest-wins as now; stamp the surface band by `d ≤ corridorWidthM/2`. Densify road splines to ≤ 5 m spacing (`geodata.ts:140`, `samplesPerSeg` from a length-based count) if you keep sample-based stamping. Then run a **max-grade filter** over each road's height profile before stamping (forward/backward pass clamping `|dh/ds| ≤ tan(22°)`, roads flattened into the hillside with `halfWidth 6, influence 60`) so the Gotthard, Klausen and Sattel roads are walkable; the pass points themselves (`gotthard`, `klausenpass`) must stay at gazetteer height. Test: along-road grade ≤ 25° everywhere on all 14 roads, `surfaceAt==='road'` on ≥ 95 % of centreline samples, and the ROADS chain through `sattel-letzi → morgarten → oberaegeri` hugs the Ägerisee shore instead of crossing water.
3. **Peaks are additive on top of the base ridge; harness cameras end up underground and render black** — evidence: `free-schoellenen.png` (camera 380 vs terrain 388.8), `free-pilatus-luzern.png` (400 vs 551.8), P2 (Pilatus 691/565, Stanserhorn 621/488, Urirotstock 957/831, Rossberg 464/382, Bürgenstock 92/231). Fix in `heightmodel.ts:129-152`: make the peak field a *target* height — `target = base(x,z) + (p.h − base(p.x,p.z)) · shape(d)` and `heights = max(heights, target)` — so `heightAt(summit) === p.h`; drop the global `shoreDamp` and instead damp only within ~150 m of a shore (Bürgenstock is a real lakeside cliff). Then remove the black-frame workarounds (`terrainMaterial.ts:297-302` grey floor; `:323-328` CSM opt-out; `terrain.ts:255` `receiveShadow=false`) and re-test the two scenarios — the "unexplained black" was the camera inside a mountain looking at back-faces. If a scenario camera is still marginal after the fix (Schöllenen: the STEEP_V wall is 60 m above the floor only 78 m east of the bridge, and a real Schöllenen has room for the mule track + bridge abutments — widen `STEEP_V.halfWidth` to ~25, `geodata.ts:102`), file `requests/world-1.md` to move the camera rather than shipping a black frame. Test: `heightAt(p)` within ±10 % of `p.h` for every landmark peak; every scenario camera in `tools/harness/scenarios.json` is ≥ 5 m above `heightAt`.
4. **Valley walls read as sheer cliffs with stretched textures and sawtooth spikes** — evidence: `free-altdorf.png` (150 m vertical wall beside the floor, conical spike row), all vistas (vertical streaking). Fix: (a) a real talus pass — extend `thermalSmooth` (`heightmodel.ts:330-353`) into a slope-limited relaxation that iterates (≥ 12 passes) until `|Δh| ≤ tan(38°) · texel` between neighbours, applied after step 4 and again after the shore pass; (b) reduce the `ridge2D` jag term (`heightmodel.ts:223`) to ≤ 3 m and gate it on rock surface, not raw slope; (c) triplanar sampling for the rock/scree group in `terrainMaterial.ts:287-292` (blend xz/xy/zy projections by |normal|) and bind the generated normal maps (`:231-233`) — SwiftShader cost is not the target, the RTX 3060 / M2 budget is. Test: fraction of map cells with slope > 60° ≤ 3 % outside the Schöllenen/Axen gorge polygons.
5. **Forests vanish beyond 900 m and distant mountains are bald grey** — evidence: far flanks in `lake-overview-seelisberg.png`, `flyover-streaming.png`. Fix `vegetation.ts:100`: keep impostors (spacing 40–50 m, count-capped) for LOD3 chunks, and darken the `forest` splat group texture (`textures.ts:101`) so forested slopes read as forest even where no instances are drawn; impostor texture at 64 px is a green triangle — a 256 px canvas with a real spruce silhouette costs nothing.
6. **Settlement pads are bare tan islands** — evidence: Bauen/Flüelen/Altdorf discs in the PNGs. After issues 1–2 the surrounding terrain will be near pad height; additionally widen the blend (`heightmodel.ts:266` `radius·0.7 → radius·1.6` with a wider outer radius) and classify the pad interior as `grass`/`meadow` except a small `settlement` core so it does not render as a sand disc before any building is placed.
7. **Region polygons overlap** — evidence: P4, 19/83 places inside ≥ 2 polygons (`geography.ts:47-66` hull inflation ×1.15 + 330 m pad; `uri-gotthard` wholly inside `alps-high`). First-match order currently gives the right answers, but move `alps-high` behind everything explicitly and add a test that every gazetteer place resolves to exactly one region, or assign by nearest region seed.
8. **Tests do not test the terrain** — `heightmodel.test.ts:88` builds 256×272; the 24 tests pass while the world is unplayable. Promote the probes above (shore step, road grade, peak height, surface census, camera-above-ground) into `src/world/*.test.ts` on the full grid (3.3 s).
9. **`chunk-loaded` / `time-changed` events are not emitted** (§5.1 "Emits") — add `ctx.events.emit` in `terrain.ts:uploadChunk` and `index.ts:setTimeOfDay`. Also warn once in `models.ts:615` when an unknown id falls back to the magenta placeholder, and add `'snow'` to `SURFACE_IDS` (`heightmodel.ts:26`) so `surfaceAt` can report the live snow line to exploration.
10. **No streaming-hitch evidence** — `flyover-streaming` cannot fly without exploration (`main.ts:220`). Not the world builder's bug, but until exploration lands, the builder should drive the camera by hand (`__harness.setCamera` in a loop) and quote `hitches` from a moving camera; `populateChunk` (`vegetation.ts:134-181`) runs synchronously on the main thread per newly active chunk (~2 000 samples × `slopeAt` with a `Vector3` allocation each) — measure it.
