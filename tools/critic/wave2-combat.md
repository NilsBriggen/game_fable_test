## (a) Is 29% AI-vs-AI a fair proxy for a human player?

No — in both directions, and the reasons matter more than the number. The auto-player is a heuristic that (i) fires caches on round 1 whether or not the column has bunched, (ii) never Shoves, never Disengages, never un-braces to close, and (iii) after the cavalry breaks, sits at `holdRange 5` while two crossbowmen shoot it to pieces. A human does better in the opening (my hold/rockfall/block script wins its wins in 10–42 rounds vs the AI's 20–51) but hits the same wall in the endgame, because the wall is not tactical: unlimited bolts, uphill kiting at equal speed (both 9 m), a knight that flees at morale < 20 and is never Routed, and a `rout` objective that demands 100 %. Reasoning through the mechanics of a *perfect* human: hold braced in two Haufen → each knight charge into a front unit eats four Brace attacks (spear 1d8+str −4 thrust soak, doubled vs mounted ≈ 3–4 each → ~12–14 per charge against ~50 hp knights) and the target's DC-12 charge check (Haufen Edge, +3 formation) is passed ~85 % of the time; caches timed for the round-4 bunching (`moraleAll −15`) rout footmen on DC-14 checks; so the cavalry and footmen break in 8–15 rounds fairly reliably. Then the human must leave the slope to catch two crossbowmen who retreat 6 cells per turn and shoot 1d10 at 55–65 % every turn with no ammo limit — the block cannot close (equal speed, difficult forest at t > 0.6), so the human either grinds 60+ rounds losing 2–4 militia to attrition or stalls into the 20-round stalemate rule, which scores as `'fled'`. **A competent human following hold/rockfall/Haufen wins the *battle* reliably (the column breaks) but does not win the *encounter* reliably.** That gap is entirely in four rules (issue 1 below), not in the player's decisions — fix those and the same human should win 70–85 %, the AI ~40–50 %.

## (b) Is the column too small to feel like Leopold's army?

Yes. Seven units (2 knights, 3 footmen, 2 crossbowmen) on a 24-cell road is a patrol, not a column "strung along the road"; the round-4 caption "The column bunches" describes nothing on screen, and the round-1 screenshot shows more militia than Habsburgs. The builder's own note says larger counts were tried and made the ratio worse — that is because every extra unit arrives at once and all of it is in play from round 1. The historical shape is *sequential*: the vanguard reaches the ambush point, gets hit, and the rest of the column piles into it from behind. Proposal (content only, no archetype changes):
- **Composition ~16**: `Duke Leopold` + 4 mounted knights, 6 footmen, 2 crossbowmen, 2 squires (`habsburg-squire` exists), + optionally the sergeant as Leopold's banner-man (`leader-down` trigger already exists — make Leopold the `group` leader so *his* rout is a morale shock to the column).
- **Arrival in waves via the existing `scripted spawn`**: place only the vanguard (2 knights, 2 footmen, 1 crossbowman) at r 4–10 at start; spawn the second wave (Leopold, 2 knights, 2 footmen, squires) at r = 0–2 on round 2, the third (knight, 2 footmen, crossbowman) on round 4 with the existing "the column bunches" caption and `moraleAll −15`. Per-round force ratio stays close to today's 7-vs-8 while 16 units cross the screen; the caches then have something to hit on rounds 3–5, which makes rockfall *timing* a real decision.
- **Terrain**: keep the two Haufen blocks and the letzi flanks; add a third cache further up-column (r ≈ 10–12, above the road) that requires a unit to *leave* the block to fire — the hold-vs-chase tension the design asks for; keep the lake passable-but-difficult so a Shove/Push-of-Pike from the road edge (q = 4) actually drowns a knight (today the road is 3 cells wide and nobody stands on q = 4).
- **End condition**: `rout` with `threshold: 0.6` (implement the schema field: encounter ends when ≥ 60 % of enemy units are dead/routed/off-map) so Leopold and a rump can "escape" as §1 requires, and the fight ends the round the column breaks instead of 90 rounds later.

## (c) Anything still "a spell with the word swapped"?

No. *Verschnaufen* (was Second Wind) is now gated on no adjacent enemy and described as a breather; War Cry is a free shout that now genuinely gives Edge on the next morale check to those who hear it; Rally is an action with a leadership requirement and a voice radius; Wall of Iron, Sure Foot and Mountain Stride now do the physical things their names say. The one thing I would still tighten: Bandage healing a *standing* ally 1d4 as a bonus action mid-melee is generous for a linen dressing — acceptable under §5.3's "HP = fighting capacity", but consider making the heal only usable on a target with no adjacent enemy, like Verschnaufen.

## Ranked issues

1. **Morgarten endgame is a 100-round attrition grind decided by unlimited-ammo kiting, not by tactics.** — evidence: `rounds median=103`, every loss `standing=Crossbowman@27-32,11-12`; human script 5/12 with the same endgame. — fix (four small rules, then re-sample): (a) decrement `ammoQty` on every ranged attack/Cover Fire and refuse `aimed-shot`/`attack`-ranged at 0 (`engine.ts:1389`, `attackInputsFor`); give crossbowmen 8–10 bolts; when empty the AI must close with the dagger or, if alone, take an `ally-down`-class morale check each round; (b) implement `rout.threshold` (`engine.ts:450`: `routedOrDown / total ≥ threshold`) and set Morgarten's to 0.6; (c) `knightAct` morale < 20 → `u.routed = true` (or a forced check at DC 14) instead of an endless flee (`ai.ts:93-97`); (d) `waldstaetteAct`: once no mounted enemy stands, drop `holdRange` to ∞ and advance as a block (`ai.ts:190`). Target: AI-vs-AI 40–60 %, p90 fight length ≤ 30 rounds; tighten `morgarten.test.ts` to assert both.
2. **Column composition/feel** — see (b): 16 units in three scripted waves, third cache up-column, `rout` threshold. Content-only.
3. **Drowning never happens and is escapable.** — fix: a `drowning` unit has `moveM = 0` unless an adjacent ally spends a bonus action to haul it out (or `Sure Foot`); AI Shove: `footman`/`waldstaette` should Shove an adjacent enemy standing on a cell whose neighbour is water/ledge (bonus action, currently never used); place the road's lake edge at q = 4 with the column's knights on it so the situation arises.
4. **Aimed shot grants no Edge; Shield block missing.** — fix: `attackInputsFor` pushes `'aimed'` when `ability.id === 'ability.aimed-shot'` and the unit has not moved this turn (`u.ap.moveM === u.ap.moveMax` before the `noMove` zeroing — capture it in `cmdAbility`); Shield block = reaction on being hit while `u.shield`, reduce 1d6 (`applyDamage` hook), AI accepts automatically.
5. **Result promise not re-armed after `restore()`** (admitted). — fix: `restore()` returns/stores a new `Promise<CombatResult>` exposed as `resume()`; document in `index.ts` that the save module's load path must not await `start()`.
6. **Brace/attack event-order anomaly** (1 of 3 seeds). — fix: add a test asserting every `reaction` record for a mover's turn precedes that mover's `attack` record; if it fails, the second `aiMove` in `moveThenAttack` is queuing braces after `tryAttack` already resolved.
7. **AI still never Disengages/Shoves and ignores OAs when moving** (admitted) — medium-low now; the flank/height/hold doctrines are in.
