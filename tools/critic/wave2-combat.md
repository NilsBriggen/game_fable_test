## Answers to the four questions

1. **Is 83 % for a mistake-free auto-player a good result?** As a number for the set piece, it is defensible — the historical outcome was decisive, and the disciplined human script lands at the same 83 %. As a *design* result it is not yet what the brief asks, because the ratio is insensitive to discipline: charger 92 %, disciplined 83 %, no-cache 58 %. The caches are the only doctrine element whose absence costs the player; holding and bracing cost more than they earn against footmen who never brace back. The Habsburg column, not the Confederate script, is the knob: give `footmanAct` the same "Brace when a polearm enemy is within 2× speed" rule the waldstätte AI has, and make the Habsburg `sergeant` Rally (already coded) reach the column, and re-measure charger vs disciplined. Expected: charger drops well below 50 %, disciplined stays ≥ 70 %.
2. **Does the fight end by rout in a sensible number of rounds?** Yes. 24/24 AI fights and 36/36 scripted fights ended; median 19 (AI) / 25 (disciplined human) / 15 (charger); p90 41; max 51. Wins end by the 60 % rout threshold with Leopold and a rump escaping; losses by the last Confederate going Down. No stalemates, no `fled` fallbacks.
3. **Does the column feel like Leopold's army?** Now yes, within a 40×24 grid: 16 named-role Habsburgs arriving in three waves along a road hemmed by lake and rock, a vanguard that gets hit first, Leopold with a banner-man in the second wave, the round-4 bunching caption with its morale shock, and a Schwyz relief body coming down the gap on round 3. The `note` is honest about the vanguard-for-column abstraction.
4. **BG3 clarity bar?** Met in the state view and overlay: turn order, AP pips, movement metres, Defense with formation breakdown, hit % with named Edge/Burden sources, objective progress, pending-reaction line, per-line attack breakdown available in `roll.breakdown`. Two clarity gaps remain for Wave 3 to consume: `abilitiesFor` must include `ability.roll-boulders` (issue 1 below) and Shield block should be a queued reaction for player units (issue 3).
