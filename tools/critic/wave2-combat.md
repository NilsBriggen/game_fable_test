The skeleton is right — a clean pure-rules layer, real per-unit initiative, an honest action economy, Edge/Burden with named sources and pairwise cancellation, soak by type, Down/bleed, OA/Disengage, Brace with double dice vs mounted, the reload ladder, a working Haufen evaluator, morale that can end a fight, seven objective types, scripted events, and 55 green tests. - **Spells with the word swapped**: `Second Wind` (`abilities.ts:108`) is the 5e Fighter feature by name and effect (bonus action, self-heal 1d6) — rename and justify (e.g. *Verschnaufen*: only when no enemy adjacent, or costs the action). `Rally` is fine (action, leadership-gated, voice radius). `War Cry` is fine in concept (free shout, once) but is currently a no-op status, and a free action that does nothing is worse than a spell. `Wall of Iron`, `Mountain Stride`, `Sure Foot` are named like feats and do nothing — hide them until implemented. ## Ranked issues (most damaging first; each with fix)

1. **Abilities ignore range/reach; melee hits from anywhere.** — evidence: Probe 1 (`attack@8 cells … hit`). — fix: in `cmdAbility` (`engine.ts:939`) and `aiAbility` (`:1173`) reject unless `target` is in `this.targets(unitId, abilityId)` (id match, or cell equality for cell/line/cone); add a test per target kind. Also make `resolveAttack` assert `cellDistance ≤ reach` defensively.
2. **Charge has no run-up precondition; combined with per-hit morale checks it makes cavalry an auto-win and Morgarten unwinnable (0/24).** — evidence: Probe 4, Probe 10b, round-1 trace. — fix: (a) `ability.charge` requires `u.mounted && u.chargeCells >= 3` and the last straight segment must end adjacent to the target; reset `chargeCells` after the attack (`engine.ts:1004`); knight AI must plan a ≥3-cell straight approach or fall back to a plain attack (`ai.ts:351-358`). (b) Rate-limit morale: at most one `damage` check per unit per round, or only on crossing 50 %/25 % HP; keep charge/rockfall/ally-down checks. (c) Give the defenders their tools: militia start `braced` when `enc.ambush === 'player'`, and the AI `waldstaetteAct` should Brace (bonus) whenever a mounted enemy is within 2× its speed. Re-sample: the target is a *human-plausible* win with ~50 % AI-vs-AI wins, not 0 %.
3. **Morgarten is not the LORE set piece.** — evidence: `haufenEver=false` 24/24, `drown=0` 24/24, no letzi feature, lake north, no Leopold, `survive 3` trivially met at the start cells. — fix in `encounters.ts:250-300`: place the 8 militia as two 2×2 Haufen blocks on the slope *above* the caches (e.g. r 10–11 at q 10–11 and q 24–25) so a Haufen exists at deployment; add `{kind:'letzi-wall', cells:[…]}` across the road's south end and make `path.ts` treat `letzi-wall` as impassable for `mounted` movers; add `{type:'hold-cells', cells:<ridge>, turns:3}` instead of bare `survive`; set `ambush:'player'` and honour `enc.ambush` in `start()` (`engine.ts:101` → `opts?.ambush ?? enc.ambush`); rotate the preset so water is at low **q** (or set yaw ≈ −π/2) to match "lake west"; add a named `habsburg-knight` (`name: 'Duke Leopold'`, `npc:'npc.leopold-i'`) with a scripted `round ≥ 8 → moves to edge / removed` so he "escapes"; make Drowning real (see 5).
4. **Flanking is computed without adjacency and counts Down units.** — evidence: Probe 3b/3c. — fix: `isFlanked` (`formation.ts:210`) takes hostiles with their reach and requires `cellDistance ≤ reach` for both; `targetIsFlanked` (`engine.ts:672`) filters `!o.down && !o.routed`. Tests for both.
5. **Statuses written but never read: Drowning, War Cry, Sure Foot, Mountain Stride, Wall of Iron, Disarmed; trunk-cache and Shield Wall unusable.** — evidence: Probe 9, greps. — fix: Drowning: at `beginUnitTurn` take 1d6, no action/bonus, `Exhausted`; at 0 HP in water → dead (this is the "knights drown" of §1); War Cry → `edge: true` in `rollMorale` when `hasStatus('war-cry')`; Disarmed → force the unarmed fallback in `attackInputsFor`; Sure Foot → skip `addStatus('prone')` in `applyFall`/`movePush`; Mountain Stride → `difficult` ignored in `dijkstra` for that mover; Wall of Iron → extend `computeBraceTriggers` extras to 2 adjacent allies. `requires.terrainFeature` → accept `string | string[]` (or use `feature.kind in {'boulder-cache','trunk-cache'}`), and count both kinds for the objective. Shield Wall → `requires: { shield: true }` checked against `u.shield`. Until each is implemented, drop it from `abilitiesFor`.
6. **Shove geometry/collision wrong.** — evidence: Probe 5 (`(5,6)→(6,7)`). — fix `movePush` (`engine.ts:1114-1115`): `dq = sign(t.q − s.q); dr = sign(t.r − s.r); if (!dq && !dr) dq = 1;` — never `|| 1` on one axis alone; on collision set Prone on *both*; require `cellDistance(attacker, target) ≤ 1` for Shove (covered by issue 1).
7. **`previewAttack`/`viewOf.defense` omit cover, so the HUD will disagree with the roll.** — evidence: Probe 6b (`view.defense 12`, `actual 13`). — fix: `effectiveDefense(u, this.cellAt(u.q,u.r)?.cover ?? 0)` in both (`engine.ts:566, 644`); compute `hitChance` exactly from `critRange`, nat-1 and the 2d20 modes (closed form, no rolling).
8. **Morale triggers and Shaken effects incomplete.** — evidence: audit table; `grep` for `'ally-down'|'leader-down'|'flanked'` → declared only. — fix: in `applyDamage` when a unit goes Down, call `checkMoraleTrigger(ally,'ally-down')` for same-side units within 3 cells; raise `'flanked'` once per round in `resolveAttack` when `flanked`; `'leader-down'` when a `group` leader (sergeant/named) goes Down; Shaken → `burden.push('shaken')` in `attackInputsFor` and clamp `moveM` to 0 toward enemies (or halve it). Fumble → `addStatus('fumbled',1)` → Burden on the next attack.
9. **Commands are not authorised.** — evidence: Probe 2. — fix: `cmdStance`/`cmdDeploy` check `activeUnitId` (deploy: `phase === 'deploy'`); introduce a real `deploy` phase when `enc.deploy` exists and a real party is present (builder-admitted gap).
10. **`advance()` loop guard emits `console.error` under a legal `auto` command.** — evidence: Probe 7. — fix: make `cmdAuto` loop `while (round < stop) advance()` with the guard reset per round; a genuinely stuck round (no unit could act) should end the encounter as `'fled'`/stalemate rather than log an error.
11. **`restore()` degrades the log and never re-arms the result promise.** — evidence: Probe 8. — fix: serialize `log` as `CombatEventRecord[]` in a private field of `objectivesState` (core `SerializedCombat.log: string[]` stays satisfied) and restore kinds/data; after `restore`, `finish()` should still `emit('end')` (it does) and the save module's load path must not await `start()` — document this in `index.ts`, or add `resume(): Promise<CombatResult>`.
12. **Renderer draws a different terrain than the rules use.** — evidence: quay PNG shows a flat grid; the preset's 1.5 m quay and 3 water rows are invisible; Morgarten PNG is a white-out with no grid. — fix: in `buildGridLines` use `combat.cellToWorld(q,r).y` (the engine's cell heights) instead of `world.heightAt`; tint cell planes by `surface` (water blue, road ochre, rock grey) and draw `feature` cells (cache = boulder pile, letzi = low wall) so the fight reads at a glance; raise the grid-line opacity and add a fog-proof emissive material so it survives winter fog; enlarge/colour the active-unit cone and outline Haufen cells more strongly.
13. **Overlay lacks the BG3 essentials that already exist in the data.** — evidence: `render.ts:329-348`. — fix: for the active unit list `previewAttack` vs the nearest enemy (`hit%`, `Edge: …`, `Burden: …`), `formation` (`+N def, Haufen #id`), `pendingReaction.trigger`, and print `roll.breakdown` lines for attack events instead of "hit."
14. **Initiative double-counts agility.** — fix: `engine.ts:232` → `die(10) + u.initiativeBonus`.
15. **AI doctrines incomplete.** — footman never flanks (score cells by `isFlanked(target, cell, allies)`), crossbowman ignores height (add `+heightDelta` to the cell score), nobody uses Brace/Disengage/Shove; AI ignores opportunity attacks when moving. Also "Second Wind" naming (see BG3 section), `ability.rally-bonus` `historical:'legend'`, and the `|| true` leftover at `engine.ts:584`.
16. **CombatEffect DSL is not interpreted.** `abilities.ts:2-5` claims the engine interprets `effects` generically; `executeAbility` switches on ids. Either interpret the DSL (damage/status/push/pull/moraleCheck/heal/reload/rally/stance/line/cone/disengage/dash/stabilize are all simple) or correct the comment and accept that content cannot add abilities without engine code — which contradicts §3.3's intent.

## Builder-admitted gaps, weighed against the bar
- **Directional cover** — low. Flat cover is enough for an 8; fix issue 7 first so the cover that exists is at least visible.
- **Deploy phase auto** — medium. Not needed for the harness, but the UI wave cannot place the party without `phase:'deploy'`, and `cmdDeploy` being callable at any time is an exploit (issue 9).
- **Riposte / Wall of Iron / Disarm / Cover fire not consumed** — Cover fire is a §5.3 reaction and the crossbowmen's only reactive play: medium. Riposte/Disarm are perk abilities: low-medium, but they are *offered* in `abilitiesFor` and Disarm silently does nothing — hide until done. Wall of Iron: low.
- **Mountain Stride / Sure Foot unread** — low individually, but together with War Cry, Drowning and Disarmed the pattern (six inert statuses) is what drops the depth score; hide or implement (issue 5).

## Explicitly out of reach for a browser engine (not counted against the score)
- SwiftShader frame times (p95 2.3–4.4 s) — software renderer; draw calls (121/369) and triangles (1.14 M/1.87 M) are within §2 budgets.
- Cinematic camera, animated attacks, environmental chain reactions, replay — 10/10 items, not required.
- The Morgarten white-out is the world module's winter/fog rendering; only the grid/terrain-tint part (issue 12) is the combat builder's.
