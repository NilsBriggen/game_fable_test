## Ranked issues (open)
N1. **Recruited companions are scheduled NPCs, not followers** — evidence: probe above (`moved 0 m`, frozen at the schedule position). In exploration a companion should trail the player 2–4 m behind; instead he stands wherever his schedule says, and `analyticPosition` will teleport him around the map on re-entry. Fix (`npc.ts` `update`): `if (this.world.has(id, PartyMember)) { this.stepFollow(id, t, playerPos, dt); continue; }` — never freeze party members, walk toward `player − 3·forward` with `walkToward`, keep the mesh, skip `stepSchedule`; on `populate()` also clear their `Npc.schedule` so re-entry logic never snaps them. One probe: after 60 s the companion is within 6 m of a moving player.
N2. **71 minor named NPCs still stand on one spot all day** — `daySchedule()` in `npcs.ts:48-56` has no offsets, so `withDefaultOffset` gives work/market/tavern the same jitter. Fix: give `minor()` the same per-activity offset generation the crowd now gets (`npc.ts:134-137`), or add `offset`s in `daySchedule()`; the innkeepers should stand at the `inn` variant house, priests at the church.
N3. **Zug is a five-house hamlet** — evidence: real-grid probe `poi.zug: {chapel:1, well:1, house.stone:5, castle.wall:9}`, `water at 60 m in 7/8 directions`. The world's Zugersee swallows the gazetteer point (`zug` h −5, inside the lake polygon), so `dryRadius(60)` finds ~40 m of land and the town shrinks to fit. Two-sided fix: exploration should search for the driest centre within 150 m (max of `dryRadius` over a 5×5 grid of candidate centres) before laying out a `town`, and the world builder should give Zug/Küssnacht a flattened dry pad ≥ 120 m (as it does for Altdorf/Schwyz/Sarnen, all `surface=settlement`, slope 0°). Same for Küssnacht (`water in 8/8 directions`, 10 houses on 44 m). Cross-module: file `requests/world-<n>.md`.
N3b. **Treib and Steinbruch am Axen have no geometry; two relocated POIs sit on cliffs** — `poi.treib(port)` centre is water on the real grid (gazetteer h 0) so `layoutPort`'s hut/cross are skipped (only 3 boats); `poi.steinbruch-axen(camp)` sits on 30° scree so campfire+tent fail the 28° gate. Fix: `layoutPort` should place the hut with `dryRadius`-style search up to 40 m inland; give `camp` a 35° allowance or move the quarry 60 m downslope. The relocated `poi.wegkreuz-axenweg` centre is on a 44° slope and `poi.fischerhuetten-gersau` on 51° (`surface=road`, h 2.5) — the models were rotated onto gentler ground by `Builder.add`, but the POI centre itself (where `nearestPoi`/markers point and where fast travel could drop a player if it were enabled) is unwalkable; nudge each 20–30 m toward the shore path and add a slope assertion to `poi-siting.test.ts` (`slopeDeg(centre) ≤ 30` for non-landmark kinds).
